❓How this dashboard works — rules & definitions

Data sources

  • Stock on Hand (NetStock) — can on-hand, per-item lead time, safety stock, and NetStock's own status (Ok / Potential s/o / Stocked out…).
  • Live PA — finished-goods on hand (Q.O.H.) and every open sales order with its ship date. INVENTORY placeholder lines (cust 9999 / year 2055) are ignored.
  • Purchase Orders — open can PO lines (ordered − received, converted THOU → cans) with required dates.
  • Can Inv Count — physical counts. If a can was Counted / Sent for Adjustment / Adjusted, the physical figure replaces the system figure (latest count date wins). Variances > 10% or 5,000 cans are flagged Δ.
  • Forecast + BOM — built in. Forecast is in cases; the BOM explodes each finished good into cans (≈12.24 cans per 12-pack case incl. scrap).
  • DSC Floor Stock — the "Alamance Detailed Inventory On Hand" pivot from DSC: can codes in column A, quantities by year/period. The current-period column (e.g. 2026-08) is read as DSC's floor on hand; the row Grand Total is kept as "all lots". If DSC's floor covers a can's full need, the quick-turn lead is applied automatically (DSC✓ chip) — which pulls that can out of urgent ordering.

Demand & timing math

PA first, forecast second. For each finished good: firm Live PA orders are consumed against FG on hand in ship-date order. The first order that FG stock cannot cover sets the production start. If no firm shortfall exists, forecast coverage (FG on hand ÷ daily forecast) sets it. Daily can burn = current + next period forecast, exploded through the BOM.
  • Runs dry — day the can balance goes negative with no new supply.
  • Need-by = runs-dry date − production buffer (default 7 days, so production always has a week of cans ahead).
  • Last day to order = need-by − lead time. Standard DSC lead is 28 days; cans on the Floor stock @ DSC list use the quick-turn lead (default 7 days) instead.
  • Run-out (with POs) — same simulation but including open PO arrivals on their required dates. Overdue POs count as arriving today but are flagged.

Status boxes

  • Stocked out — zero cans, consumption already running, nothing arriving within 3 days.
  • Expedite PO — a PO exists but its first arrival lands after the need-by date: pull the PO in rather than ordering again.
  • Order today — last safe order day is today or already past.
  • This week / Next week / Upcoming — last safe order day in 1–7 / 8–14 / 15–28 days (or a NetStock flag).
  • Covered — no action inside the horizon.

Priority queue

All urgent cans ranked by the earliest firm PA order at risk (chip shows P8 PA / P9 PA); forecast-only cans rank after all PA-driven ones. The queue drives both swaps and the PO plan.

Stale backorders

Two kinds of PO lines are ignored entirely — excluded from incoming supply, coverage, run-out math and swaps: (1) stale backorders, overdue by more than Ignore POs overdue days (default 3); and (2) short-ship remnants, lines with less than Ignore PO remnants cans left open (default 5,000) — the small leftovers when DSC delivers slightly under the ordered quantity. Both appear in the "Ignored POs" box with a reason, ready to be verified and cleared from the system.

Swap rules

A swap is only suggested when it is meaningful:
  • The urgent can must have a firm PA shortfall — forecast-only demand never takes a swap slot.
  • The urgent need must be ≥ Min swap qty (default 20,000 cans) — no swapping for 2–3k-can needs.
  • The donor PO sits on a can that is fully covered (no production need in 180 days) or arrives ≥ 14 days early, is itself ≥ Min swap qty, and is due before the urgent can's need-by.
  • Each donor PO can be claimed by only one urgent can. Remaining need after a swap flows into the PO plan.

PO builder rules

  • Every PO is one full truck (default 95,000 cans).
  • Every line is ≥ MOQ 5,000 and always a multiple of 5,000 — these rules are never broken.
  • Needs are packed in queue priority order; one item can span multiple POs, and one PO can mix items.
  • A short final truck is topped up by pulling next week's needs forward (lines tagged PULLED FWD). If nothing can be pulled, the truck is marked PARTIAL — top it up or hold it.
  • POs to place now = contain items whose last order day is ≤ 2 days out; next week = 3–14 days.
  • Expedite items are excluded (their fix is the existing PO); swap-covered quantity is deducted first.

Swap decisions — the DSC round-trip

Swaps here are proposals until DSC agrees. Export the Swap decision template from the swaps section, have DSC mark each row YES or NO in the Decision column, and drop the file back onto the board (✅ Swap Decisions tile). YES rows lock in and show ✔ CONFIRMED BY DSC. NO rows stay visible with a red ✖ NO — DECLINED BY DSC indicator: the board then recommends the next-best donor PO as an ↻ ALTERNATIVE PO (clearly marked, with the declined PO named on the card), or — if no other covered PO qualifies — the urgent can's need automatically flows into the new-PO plan, where the line is tagged ⇄ swap declined by DSC → new PO so you can trace exactly where it went. A declined donor PO is never proposed again, even across repeated decision uploads (decisions merge, they don't overwrite). Blank rows stay proposed. Removing the file (✕ on the tile) returns everything to proposed.

Customer filter

Pick one or more customers (from Live PA order lines) in the 👥 dropdown. The whole page then shows only cans tied to those customers' firm orders, and the queue, swaps and PO plan are ranked by their orders. Forecast burn rates stay whole underneath so run-out dates remain realistic. Clear returns to all customers.

On-hand policy — days of supply (all customers)

At the top set the global Min days (floor), Ideal days (the target we order up to), and Max days (ceiling) of supply. Every can is planned to these unless a customer or can overrides them: order sizing aims for the Ideal days and never exceeds Max; anything below Min is flagged.

⬇ Export policy (in the floor-stock row) downloads a simple file with one row per customer and four columns: Customer, Ideal days of supply, Minimum days of supply, Maximum days of supply. Edit the numbers, save, and ⬆ Import policy — every can that serves a customer is re-planned to that customer's days-of-supply policy. Leave a customer blank to use the generic top-of-dashboard defaults. Co-manufacturing customers default to the tighter co-man band. Standard lead time (28d), buffer (7d) and cover still apply.

Co-manufacturing customers — low-inventory band

Some customers (default Conagra, Rubix, Danone, Nestle) are co-manufacturing accounts where we deliberately hold very little stock. In the 🤝 bar up top you can add or remove customers (matched by name, case-insensitive) and set the Min and Max on-hand days (default 7 and 14). Any can that feeds one of these customers' finished goods is flagged 🤝 CO-MAN and held to that tight band: its order sizing uses the MAX-day window as its cover instead of the standard cover, so it's never overstocked, and it's still ordered a week ahead. The 🤝 Co-manufacturing section shows every co-man can with its on-hand, current days-of-supply and band, colour-coded BELOW MIN / IN BAND / OVER MAX. Standard 28-day lead time is unchanged; floor-stock cans follow their own quick-turn rule.

⬇ Co-man on-hand plan exports one row per customer × can with on-hand, daily use, days of supply, and the MIN/MAX day band (and the equivalent MIN/MAX on-hand in cans). Edit the MIN/MAX day columns for any can, save, and ⬆ Import adjusted bands — those per-can bands override the global default and the board re-plans instantly.

Everything exports

Every box, the queue, the swap list, the PO plan and every single can exports to Excel with the action and full reasoning included.

Consumption Planner page

The 🗓 Consumption Planner tab (top-left) breaks the same live production need down by fiscal week or fiscal period (2026 calendar: P1–P12, 4/5-week periods, embedded). It auto-detects today's period and week, highlights the current bucket, and shows per-can consumption, incoming POs and the projected end balance per bucket — red when a shortfall is projected. The current bucket counts remaining days only. Search and the customer filter apply here too, and the whole grid exports to Excel.

Lead times, buffer, truck size, MOQ, min-swap and the floor-stock list are all editable at the top — the whole board recalculates instantly.
